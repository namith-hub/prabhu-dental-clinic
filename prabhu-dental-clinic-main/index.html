About Prabhu Dental Clinic

Continuing a Legacy of Dental Care Since 2012

Dedicated patient care, ethical practice, and continuous clinical betterment in Saraswathipuram, Mysore.

"Clinic is owned by husband and wife duo Dr. Mahesh H.V. M.D.S. (Periodontist & Implantologist) and Dr. Ramya K.S. M.D.S. (Orthodontist and dentofacial orthopaedist). With the blessings of Dr. M.G. Prabhu, Dr. Mahesh and Dr. Ramya have taken over the clinic from him in the year 2012 and are continuing the same good work while aiming for further betterment and upgradation of the clinic in all ways for the benefit of the patients.

Carrying forward this trusted heritage in Saraswathipuram, Mysore, the clinic combines advanced clinical expertise with a gentle, patient-centric approach. Together, they offer comprehensive specialized dental care—from intricate periodontal therapies and implant restorations to modern orthodontic smile alignment—utilizing state-of-the-art dental technology, rigorous sterilization protocols, and ethical treatment standards designed for long-term oral health."

* Prabhu Dental Clinic is registered under the Karnataka Private Medical Establishments (KPMEA) Act, adhering strictly to recognized clinical, hygiene, and ethical healthcare standards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Dental FAQs & Care Insights

Answers to common queries regarding treatments, orthodontic aligners, implants, and routine dental wellness.

It is recommended to have a professional dental checkup and routine cleaning every 6 months. Regular examinations allow early detection of tooth decay, gingival bleeding, or periodontal issues before they progress into complicated conditions.
Dental implant procedures are performed under local anesthesia with minimal discomfort. With advanced 3D planning and gentle surgical techniques, recovery is fast. With proper daily oral hygiene and periodic checkups, dental implants are permanent solutions that last a lifetime.
Traditional braces use ceramic or metallic brackets and wires to gently shift teeth. Clear aligners are custom-made, transparent, and removable plastic trays that straighten teeth discreetly. Both are highly effective; Dr. Ramya K.S. (Orthodontist) will evaluate your smile and recommend the ideal option.
Bleeding gums are typically an early sign of gingivitis or accumulated plaque and tartar beneath the gumline. If ignored, it may develop into periodontitis. Professional scaling and periodontal evaluation by Dr. Mahesh H.V. (Periodontist) will restore healthy, firm gums.
Modern root canal treatments are virtually pain-free thanks to effective local anesthesia and computerized rotary endodontics. An RCT removes infected pulp, thoroughly disinfects the root canals, and seals the tooth, allowing you to preserve your original natural tooth for decades under a protective crown.
